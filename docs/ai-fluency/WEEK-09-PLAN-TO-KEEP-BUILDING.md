## How I will add the next case study

My next case study will be added to the `projects` list in `src/App.tsx`, reusing the existing project-card component and visual styles. I will write it using the Week 2 three-beat structure:

1. **Problem:** explain the need, constraint, or opportunity.
2. **What I did:** describe what I built, the decisions I made, and the tools I used.
3. **What came of it:** show the result, impact, evidence, and lessons learned.

I will then add the relevant links, technologies, and final screenshots; test the new card on desktop and mobile; and publish the update through the existing GitHub and Vercel workflow.
