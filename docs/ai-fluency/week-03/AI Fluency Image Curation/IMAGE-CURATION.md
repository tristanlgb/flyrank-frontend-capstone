# Image curation — Week 3

## Keepers

- `../AI Fluency Decide Once Build Your Identity Kit/IDENTITY-KIT.png` — real capture of the identity kit used to establish the portfolio's visual system: Space Grotesk + Inter, purple primary, near-black text, neutral background, and restrained green success accent.
- `../../PORTFOLIO-SITEMAP.svg` — real artifact showing the content structure and the screens the portfolio needs to support.

These two images map directly to real portfolio needs: visual consistency and information architecture. They are preferred over AI stand-ins because they document the actual work.

## Continuity with the final portfolio

The identity-kit capture remains a keeper because it proves the Week 3
decision, even though the final palette later evolved from purple to cream,
green, and orange. Keeping the earlier artifact is more honest than editing it
to resemble the finished site. The final system preserved the important
criteria behind the image—limited colors, readable type, and a simple
monogram—while changing the mood to better connect development with
communication and social research.

## Rejected direction

I rejected a generic AI-generated “hero portrait” direction. It looked polished but did not show real work, did not prove anything about the portfolio, and would have introduced a second visual mood unrelated to the identity kit. A decorative texture may be generated later only if it stays within the existing near-black / purple / minimal mood.

## Real-person decision

No portrait was included at this stage because a real photo of the portfolio
owner was not available in the project assets. I deliberately left the slot
open rather than using an AI substitute. A later portfolio version added a real
portrait, but the Week 7 audit found that its source resolution was too small
for a crisp high-density mobile display. A higher-resolution real photograph
remains preferable to an AI-generated replacement.
