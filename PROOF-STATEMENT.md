# What Are You Proving?

## Proof statement

I can build maintainable frontend interfaces with React and TypeScript. This portfolio is for a hiring manager at a technology company who is looking for a junior frontend developer and wants evidence of how a candidate organizes real work, documents decisions, and improves through review. Through my FlyRank capstone and its visible development history, I want to show that I can turn requirements into clear, reviewable frontend work. The single action I want that hiring manager to take is to contact me for a junior frontend interview.

## Why this needs to exist

A CV or LinkedIn profile can list React and TypeScript, but it cannot show how I organize a repository, explain my decisions, and review AI-assisted changes before accepting them.

## Interview decisions

- **Primary claim:** I can build maintainable frontend interfaces with React and TypeScript.
- **One person:** A technology-company hiring manager recruiting a junior frontend developer.
- **One action:** Contact me for a junior frontend interview.
