## Submission note

I used AI as an audit partner to review my live portfolio at phone, tablet, and desktop widths. The audit checked overflow, touch-target sizes, text readability, image dimensions, internal navigation, case-study routes, live demos, and repository links.

The portfolio has no horizontal overflow at the tested widths. Body text remains 16 px with a 24 px line height, and interactive links have at least a 44 px-high touch area. All public portfolio pages, case studies, live demos, and repository links opened during the audit.

The audit also found one honest remaining issue: the portrait source is smaller than its rendered mobile size and may look soft on a high-density screen. I documented it in the fix log instead of hiding it.

Before posting this submission, I will open the audited Week 7 URL on a
physical phone, test its navigation and email action, and attach the phone
screenshot required by the assignment. A resized browser audit supports the
fix log but does not replace the required real-device evidence.
